Sources

Pallot keeps a copy of what each source sends back, so looking up the same address again doesn't ask anyone. Turn a source off to build your ballot without it. Your choices are saved in this browser, so they change only your ballot, not anyone else's. Whoever runs Pallot refreshes the saved copies on the server.

Ballotpedia's ballot and address search are unofficial endpoints meant for personal use. Whoever shares Pallot with anyone else should turn "Ballotpedia" and "Address suggestions" off for everyone, in the sources.toml in Pallot's data folder.

Data

Your picks, notes, write-ins and pick rule are kept only in this browser, with your last address and your choices on this page and the ballot. The server keeps the sources' saved copies, which only whoever runs Pallot can clear. Privacy lists everything Pallot keeps.

  • Clear my picks & notes

    Removes your picks, notes, write-ins and pick rule. Keeps your address and your choices. Undo puts them back for a few seconds.

  • Clear browser data

    Removes everything Pallot keeps in this browser: your picks and notes, address, appearance, search engine, sources and view choices. Your sources go back to Pallot's defaults. Undo puts it back for a few seconds.
