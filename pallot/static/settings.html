How much of the ballot shows

A street map of your districts, under Your districts. Nothing is fetched for it while it's folded.

Each race's money box (folded, it's one line, with Compare funding) and the Funding line under each candidate's name, worked out from their campaign's reports: green for small donors or Texas donors, amber for large donations, out-of-state donors or self-funding, "Mostly" over half and "Overwhelmingly" over three quarters, then what they raised and the outside spending for and against them.

The poll bar in U.S. Senate, U.S. House and Governor races. Folded, it's one line.

The Endorsements line under each candidate's name: green for an endorsement (TrackAIPAC, Vote for Peace's Ally, an endorsement list), amber against (TrackAIPAC's watchlist, Vote for Peace's Opposed). Hidden, a button counts them and opens them.

The fine print under When to vote and Your districts, naming their sources. Hidden, a "Where these come from" link shows it; either way, the link opens or closes it.

    Clear data

    Your picks, notes, address and choices are kept only in this browser. Privacy lists everything Pallot keeps.

    • Clear my picks & notes

      Removes your picks, notes, write-ins and pick rule. Keeps your address and your choices. Undo puts them back for a few seconds.

    • Clear browser data

      Removes everything Pallot keeps in this browser: your picks and notes, address, appearance, search engine, sources, view choices and ban list. Your sources go back to Pallot's defaults. Undo puts it back for a few seconds.
