Sources

Pallot keeps a copy of what each source sends back, so looking up the same address again doesn't ask anyone. Turn a source off to build your ballot without it, refresh it to fetch its data again now, or clear its copy.

Ballotpedia's ballot and address search are unofficial endpoints meant for personal use; turn off "Ballotpedia" and "Address suggestions" before sharing Pallot with anyone else.

Data

Your picks, notes, write-ins and pick rule are kept only in this browser, with your last address and your choices on this page and the ballot. The server keeps the sources' saved copies. Privacy lists everything Pallot keeps.

  • Clear my picks & notes

    Removes your picks, notes, write-ins and pick rule. Keeps your address and your choices. Undo puts them back for a few seconds.

  • Clear browser data

    Removes everything Pallot keeps in this browser: your picks and notes, address, appearance, search engine and view choices. Keeps what the server saved. Undo puts it back for a few seconds.

  • Clear all source caches

    Removes everything the server saved from the sources, and puts the bundled snapshots back as they came with Pallot. Keeps everything in this browser. Can't be undone.

  • Clear all my data

    Clears this browser's data and the source caches together. Undo puts back only what this browser kept; the server's copies can't be undone.
