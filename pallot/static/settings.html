    Your data

    Your picks, notes, address and choices are kept only in this browser. An election's picks and notes are forgotten 90 days after it; to keep a copy or move them, save everything. Privacy lists everything Pallot keeps.

    • Save everything

      Saves a Pallot file with everything kept here: your picks for every election, notes, address, districts and settings. Open it on another device, or here later, to pick up where you left off.

    • Open a Pallot file

      Puts everything you saved back, replacing what's here or merged with it, with Undo, and opens your ballot where you left off.

    • Clear my picks & notes

      Removes your picks, notes, write-ins and Help me pick choices. Keeps your address and your choices. Pallot asks first, and Undo puts them back for a few seconds.

    • Clear browser data

      Removes everything Pallot keeps in this browser: your picks and notes, address, appearance, search engine, sources, view choices and the people you avoid. Your sources go back to Pallot's defaults. Pallot asks first, and Undo puts it back for a few seconds.
