About Pallot

A personal helper for working through your ballot: every race, what several sources say about each candidate, and a printout of your picks.

What it does

  • Shows every race on your ballot, in ballot order, from your home address. It covers Texas for now.
  • Puts what each source says about a candidate side by side: their money, polls and endorsements.
  • Shows when to vote and your districts, with a map.
  • Lets you pick, take notes, and print your picks to take to the polls.

What it isn't

  • Not official. It isn't tied to any government, party, campaign or source, and it can be wrong. Check your county's sample ballot.
  • Not an endorsement. It shows what the sources say about everyone on your ballot. Who you vote for is up to you.
  • Not where you register or vote. Vote.gov points you to your state's election office.

How it works

Our server asks the sources for you and keeps a copy of each answer, so the same lookup never asks twice. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and we don't sell anything. Your picks stay in your browser: we never see them.
