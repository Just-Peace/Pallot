        Your ballot, race by race

        Enter your home address in the left pane to see every race you'll vote in.

        1. Compare sources. Each candidate shows what the state's filings, Ballotpedia and TrackAIPAC say, plus a Google search.
        2. Pick and take notes. Choose a candidate in each race and jot down why. Collapse races as you go.
        3. Print your picks and take the sheet with you to vote.

        Your ballot

        VoteBot is an unofficial helper. Always check the official sample ballot from your county elections office. Data from the US Census, state election and legislative offices, Ballotpedia and TrackAIPAC; each candidate's details say which source said what.

        Print my picks

        Bring the printout with you: many polling places don't allow phones in the voting booth. To keep a copy, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
