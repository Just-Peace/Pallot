Skip to the ballot

Currently covers Texas addresses. Your address is sent to the US Census geocoder (and its location to Ballotpedia, if it's on) to find your districts, and is remembered in this browser. As you type, it's also sent to Photon for suggestions, unless they're off in Settings. More in Privacy.

    Your ballot, race by race

    Enter your home address to see every race you'll vote in.

    1. Compare sources. Each candidate shows how much money they've raised and from whom, what the state's filings, Ballotpedia and TrackAIPAC say, and where they stand in public polls, plus a web search.
    2. Pick and take notes. Choose a candidate in each race and jot down why. Collapse races as you go.
    3. Know the dates and your districts. The top of the ballot has the last day to register, early voting and Election Day, to add to your calendar, and your districts and election precinct, with a map of them.
    4. Print your picks and take the sheet with you to vote.

    Looking up your ballot… The first lookup of an address can take several seconds while VoteBot asks its sources. After that it's saved, so looking it up again is instant.
