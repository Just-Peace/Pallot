Your address

Currently covers Texas addresses. Your address is sent to the US Census geocoder (and its location to Ballotpedia, if it's on) to find your districts, and is remembered in this browser. As you type, it's also sent to Photon for suggestions, unless they're off in Settings. More in Privacy.

    Your ballot, race by race

    Enter your home address in the left pane to see every race you'll vote in.

    1. Compare sources. Each candidate shows what the state's filings, Ballotpedia and TrackAIPAC say, how much money they've raised and from whom, plus a web search.
    2. Pick and take notes. Choose a candidate in each race and jot down why. Collapse races as you go.
    3. Print your picks and take the sheet with you to vote.

    Your ballot

    VoteBot is an unofficial helper. Always check the official sample ballot from your county elections office. Data from the US Census, state election and legislative offices, Ballotpedia, TrackAIPAC, the FEC and the Texas Ethics Commission; each candidate's details say which source said what.

    Print my picks

    Bring the printout with you: many polling places don't allow phones in the voting booth. To keep a copy, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
