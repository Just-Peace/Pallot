Sources

VoteBot keeps a copy of what each source sends back, so looking up the same address again doesn't ask anyone. Turn a source off to build your ballot without it, refresh it to fetch its data again now, or clear its copy.

Ballotpedia's endpoint is unofficial and meant for personal use; turn it off before sharing VoteBot with anyone else.

In this browser

Your picks, notes and write-ins are kept only in this browser. It also remembers your last address, so your ballot is there when you come back, your search engine, and view choices such as collapsing a race when you pick. Privacy lists everything VoteBot keeps.

"Clear my picks & notes" keeps your address. "Clear all browser data" also forgets your address, search engine and view choices, as if you'd never used VoteBot in this browser. Both happen at once, with Undo for a few seconds. Neither touches what the server saved; that's under Sources.
